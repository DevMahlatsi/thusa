# Product

## Problem
Fee-based micro-businesses (crèches first) have fixed costs but late, partial income. Many parents
are paid weekly or from several small income sources, while fees are billed monthly.
ECD Census 2021: 42,420 programmes, ~70% rely mainly on fees (avg ~R509/child/month), 40% registered.

## Users
- Owner: woman 30–60, 10–60 children, smartphone, banked, collects by cash/EFT/WhatsApp today.
- Parent: low to lower-middle income, any bank, WhatsApp/SMS, often paid weekly.

## Key features
- Flexible rhythm: monthly fee split weekly/fortnightly, timed to payer's payday
- Pay from any bank via payment link; optional DebiCheck debit order
- Account verification before collecting
- Instant confirmation + auto-matching
- Polite reminders (WhatsApp/SMS) in English, isiZulu, Sesotho, Setswana
- Part-payments with tracked balance
- "Who's paid" dashboard: expected vs received
- Auto set-aside for savings/tax (FNB 3PIM, later)
- Income record from history/statements for subsidies, registration, credit
- Low data, accessible (large text, +/− signs not colour alone), scam-safe messages

## Pricing
Starter free (≤5 payers) · Standard R99/month (≤50) · Plus R199/month.
2.5% per successful collection (min R2, cap R15). Parents pay nothing.
